# Privacy Policy for Fast Video Merger **Effective Date: October 2, 2026** Fast Video Merger is a desktop application developed by Chandru Y. This Privacy Policy explains how Fast Video Merger handles user information and files. ## 1. Information We Collect Fast Video Merger does not collect, store, sell, or share users' personal information. The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its core features. ## 2. Video and Media Files Fast Video Merger processes video and other media files selected by the user locally on the user's Windows device. Your video files are not uploaded to our servers or transmitted to us. The application does not access or share files except when necessary to perform operations requested by the user. ## 3. Internet and Online Services Fast Video Merger does not require an internet connection for its core video-merging functionality. The application does not intentionally transmit user media files or personal information to external servers. ## 4. Third-Party Components Fast Video Merger may use third-party multimedia processing components, such as FFmpeg, to process video and audio files locally on the user's device. These components are used to provide the application's video-processing functionality. ## 5. Data Security Because media processing is performed locally on the user's device, users retain control of their video and media files. Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security and backups for files stored on their device. ## 6. Children's Privacy Fast Video Merger does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. ## 7. Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties. We do not upload users' video files to external servers. ## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be made available through the application's associated website or Microsoft Store listing. ## 9.
